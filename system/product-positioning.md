# FreeIndexer Product Positioning

## Product Summary

FreeIndexer is an online indexing product and desktop app for submitting URLs
and backlinks into search discovery workflows. It helps users who publish,
manage, or build many URLs reduce manual submission work and improve the chance
that important pages and backlinks are discovered by search engines.

## Primary Value Proposition

FreeIndexer gives SEO and webmaster operators a repeatable URL and backlink
submission workflow so they can prioritize important assets, submit them in
bulk, track their indexing work, and avoid manual one-by-one submission.

## ICP Reassessment

FreeIndexer should speak to one broad primary buyer: the hands-on website,
webmaster, or SEO operator responsible for getting important URLs, backlinks,
and content assets discovered. That operator shows up in several segments, but
the underlying job is the same: choose what deserves visibility, remove obvious
blockers, submit in a repeatable workflow, and report honestly.

The content system should avoid writing generic SEO advice for everyone, but it
should not be limited to FreeIndexer or URL indexing product articles. Each
article still needs one primary ICP, but the blog can cover the full practical
niche around search discovery: SEO basics, Google Search Console, crawlability,
sitemaps, internal links, backlinks, technical SEO, bulk URL operations, and
emerging AI/GEO visibility where it helps the same operator.

## ICP Pain Points

- New pages take too long to appear in Google.
- Backlinks are created but not discovered quickly.
- Agencies need a cheap repeatable workflow for client URLs.
- Affiliate marketers need money pages and supporting links discovered faster.
- Programmatic SEO builders publish many pages and need a submission process.
- SaaS teams publish docs, landing pages, changelogs, and integration pages that should be discovered quickly.
- Webmasters need repeatable Search Console, sitemap, robots, and internal
  linking checks.
- Users are comparing indexing tools, desktop apps, APIs, SEO platforms, and
  manual Google Search Console submission.

## Commercial Angles

- Free online usage and registration path.
- Low-cost monthly plans for higher daily points.
- Desktop app one-time purchase for users who prefer local software and bulk workflows.
- Support and lifecycle content can reduce confusion around plans, downloads, points, and indexing expectations.

## Differentiators To Emphasize

- Online app plus desktop app path.
- Low entry pricing.
- Built for backlink and URL indexing workflows.
- Useful for bulk submissions and repeat workflows.
- Points-based plans that map to different indexing actions.

## Claims Guardrails

Use:

- "submit URLs for faster discovery"
- "create a repeatable indexing workflow"
- "help search engines discover URLs and backlinks"
- "reduce manual indexing work"

Avoid unless validated:

- "guaranteed indexing"
- "100% indexed"
- exact submission network size
- exact indexing time promises
- ranking improvement promises
