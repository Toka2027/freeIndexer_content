# FreeIndexer Keyword Scoring

Use this score before adding a new article to the roadmap.

## Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Search value | Does the query reflect real search demand or a common indexing pain? | 1 to 5 |
| ICP fit | Does the query map clearly to one FreeIndexer ICP? | 1 to 5 |
| Business value | Can the article naturally route to online app, pricing, or desktop app? | 1 to 5 |
| Differentiation | Can FreeIndexer say something more useful than generic SEO posts? | 1 to 5 |
| Cluster value | Does the article strengthen a hub or internal linking path? | 1 to 5 |

## Priority Bands

- 22 to 25: create in the next batch.
- 18 to 21: create after the current hub has its pillar and strongest clusters.
- 14 to 17: keep as backlog unless it supports support reduction.
- Below 14: skip or reframe around a sharper ICP.

## Keyword Rules

- Prefer queries with visible pain: "not indexing", "free indexer", "bulk indexing", "backlink indexer".
- Prefer ICP modifiers over generic keywords: "for SEO agencies", "for affiliate sites", "for programmatic SEO".
- Avoid cannibalization: do not create multiple articles targeting the same primary keyword with only small wording changes.
- Do not create claims-led keywords that force unsupported promises, such as guaranteed indexing or exact time guarantees.
