# FreeIndexer Editorial Policy

## Purpose

FreeIndexer content should help legitimate site owners and SEO operators build better URL discovery workflows. It should be commercially useful without overclaiming search engine outcomes.

## Tone

Use direct, practical SEO language:

- indexing workflow
- URL discovery
- backlink discovery
- bulk submission
- crawl and index signals
- indexing delays
- plan limits
- desktop indexing workflow

## Claim Rules

Do not state that FreeIndexer guarantees Google indexing or ranking outcomes.

When discussing results, use careful language:

- "helps submit URLs for discovery"
- "can support faster discovery"
- "should be combined with crawlable pages, internal links, and quality signals"
- "indexing depends on search engine decisions"

## Risk Rules

Avoid spam-forward framing. Do not position the product as a way to manipulate rankings, mass spam links, or evade search engine quality systems.

Acceptable framing:

- indexing legitimate pages
- submitting owned URLs and known backlinks
- agency workflows for client-owned assets
- operational SEO and discovery processes

Restricted framing:

- guaranteed rankings
- spam blasts
- churn-and-burn tactics
- search engine manipulation promises
- fake authority claims

## Review States

Use `editorial_review` in frontmatter:

- `standard`: normal article workflow
- `needs-review`: sensitive SEO claims, competitor comparisons, or scale claims
- `manual-only`: legal, risky, or unsupported claims that need human approval before drafting
