# FreeIndexer Topical Map

## Content Strategy Rule

The blog serves the full FreeIndexer ICP: hands-on website owners, webmasters,
SEO operators, SEO agencies, affiliate marketers, programmatic SEO builders,
blog/network owners, and SaaS/product teams.

FreeIndexer should appear where it solves the workflow problem: after the
article has helped the reader diagnose the issue, prioritize work, and choose a
practical operating process.
