# FreeIndexer Business Goals

Every article must serve at least one business goal.

## Goal 1: Convert Indexing-Delay Demand

Capture users searching for why Google is not indexing their page, backlink, or new website.

- Target ICP: website owners, affiliate marketers, SaaS/product teams
- CTA: Try FreeIndexer or register for daily points
- Content types: troubleshooting, beginner guides, use-case guides

## Goal 2: Convert Agency And Bulk Workflow Demand

Capture SEO agencies and operators who need a repeatable URL submission process for many client URLs.

- Target ICP: SEO agencies, blog/network owners, programmatic SEO builders
- CTA: Compare plans or start with a paid plan
- Content types: agency workflows, bulk indexing guides, point planning

## Goal 3: Capture Comparison And Switching Demand

Win users comparing indexing tools, indexing APIs, desktop apps, and manual submission.

- Target ICP: technical SEO operators, agencies, tool evaluators
- CTA: View pricing or choose the online app vs desktop app path
- Content types: comparisons, buying guides, alternatives

## Goal 4: Support Desktop App Revenue

Educate users who prefer local Windows software for repeated backlink and URL indexing workflows.

- Target ICP: affiliate marketers, agencies, blog/network owners
- CTA: Buy or download the desktop app
- Content types: desktop workflow articles, setup guides, bulk use cases

## Goal 5: Reduce Support Load

Answer common questions about points, submissions, indexing expectations, plan limits, downloads, and login/dashboard usage.

- Target ICP: existing users and trial users
- CTA: Log in, check dashboard, or contact support
- Content types: troubleshooting, FAQ, support explainers
