# FreeIndexer Blog Series Map

## Strategy Rule

The blog expands from indexing education into a practical digital marketing
library, but the core product boundary stays clear:

- FreeIndexer is the indexing, URL discovery, backlink discovery, prioritization,
  bulk submission, and repeatable workflow layer.
- SEO providers such as SEOeStore belong in the provider or managed campaign
  layer.
- SEO software and automation tools belong in the execution or campaign tooling
  layer.
- Analytics, content marketing, CRO, and operations articles should help the
  reader make better decisions even if no product is used.

## Series 1: Website Visibility Foundations

Target ICP: website owner, webmaster

Business goal: build top-funnel trust with beginners who need discoverability
basics before they evaluate tools.

Funnel stage: top

Category: `webmaster-guides`

Article sequence:

1. `seo-basics-for-website-owners`
2. `how-search-visibility-works`
3. `url-indexing-vs-ranking`
4. `sitemap-and-robots-basics`
5. `google-search-console-basics`
6. `internal-linking-basics-for-website-owners`
7. `content-visibility-checklist`

Internal linking logic: the series hub links to all beginner articles; each
cluster links back to the hub and then to the most relevant indexing education
or troubleshooting guide.

CTA logic: invite the reader to build a weekly visibility checklist, then use a
submission workflow only for priority URLs.

Where FreeIndexer fits: priority URL submission and follow-up after the site is
accessible, linked, and sitemap-ready.

SEOeStore/provider fit: usually not needed except in later articles about
outsourcing SEO maintenance.
