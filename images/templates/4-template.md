# Template 4: Desktop App Workspace

## Best Article Types

- desktop app guides
- Windows software articles
- blog/network owner workflows
- product setup and download content

## Title Zone

Left 40% or lower-left title zone. Keep title readable and avoid covering the app window subject.

## Image / Subject Zone

Right and center zone showing a desktop app window, URL list, campaign start button, or local indexing workspace concept.

## Visual Mood

Practical and product-led. It should feel like software that helps the reader process many URLs with less manual work.

## When To Use It

Use this for:

- `freeindexer-desktop-app-guide`
- `windows-url-indexing-software`
- `desktop-indexer-for-blog-networks`
- `freeindexer-online-vs-desktop-app`

## When Not To Use It

Avoid this template for pure indexing education articles or API/tool comparison articles.
