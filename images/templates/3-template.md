# Template 3: Decision Matrix

## Best Article Types

- comparisons
- buying guides
- tool evaluation articles
- API vs tool decision content

## Title Zone

Left or top-left zone with a crisp title. Leave room for a small comparison badge such as `Comparison` or `Buying Guide`.

## Image / Subject Zone

Right side should show a decision matrix, side-by-side cards, workflow fork, or dashboard comparison concept.

## Visual Mood

Analytical, neutral, and buyer-oriented. The image should help readers feel that the article compares options fairly.

## When To Use It

Use this for:

- `best-google-indexing-tools`
- `freeindexer-vs-indexing-api`
- future alternatives and pricing comparison articles

## When Not To Use It

Do not use it for support articles or simple how-to articles where a matrix visual would distract from the fix.
