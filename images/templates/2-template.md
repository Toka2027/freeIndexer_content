# Template 2: Queue And Workflow

## Best Article Types

- SEO agency workflows
- bulk indexing workflows
- programmatic SEO articles
- daily points and batching guides

## Title Zone

Top-left or left-center title zone. Keep title concise and use strong line breaks around the ICP or workflow phrase.

## Image / Subject Zone

Large right-side or lower-right zone showing URL rows, queues, progress states, backlink nodes, or batch submission flow.

## Visual Mood

Operational and scalable. It should feel like a production workflow, not a generic marketing illustration.

## When To Use It

Use this for:

- `url-indexing-for-seo-agencies`
- `programmatic-seo-indexing-workflow`
- `indexing-workflow-for-affiliate-sites`

## When Not To Use It

Avoid this template for beginner troubleshooting where a queue-heavy visual may feel too advanced.
