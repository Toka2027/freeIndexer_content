## Search Promise

Give the reader a diagnostic checklist and a clear next step.

## Reader Scenario

The reader has already published a URL and may have waited days or weeks. They want to know whether the problem is technical, quality-related, discovery-related, or just delay.

## Article Angle

Troubleshoot first, submit second. This builds trust and avoids presenting FreeIndexer as a cure for pages that are blocked, noindexed, canonicalized away, or thin.

## Required Sections

1. Quick answer: common reasons a URL is not indexed.
2. Check if the URL is accessible.
3. Check noindex, robots.txt, canonical, redirects, and status codes.
4. Check internal links and sitemap inclusion.
5. Check whether the page has enough value to be indexed.
6. Submit the URL after blockers are fixed.
7. How FreeIndexer fits into the final discovery step.
8. FAQ about timing, guarantees, and repeated submissions.
