## Search Promise

Help programmatic SEO builders prioritize large page sets so URL submission capacity supports the publishing workflow.

## Reader Scenario

The reader publishes many pages from templates, databases, or location/category combinations. Their problem is not one URL; it is the operational load of many URLs.

## Article Angle

Do not tell readers to submit everything blindly. Teach them to tier URLs by business value, quality, internal links, crawl readiness, and refresh cadence.

## Required Sections

1. Why programmatic SEO creates indexing bottlenecks.
2. URL tiering model: revenue pages, category pages, long-tail pages, refreshed pages.
3. Pre-indexing quality gate.
4. Batching and daily point planning.
5. How FreeIndexer fits into the workflow.
6. When an API-style workflow may be needed vs online/desktop tool.
7. FAQ about large sites, crawl budget, and indexing expectations.
