## Search Promise

This hub explains why URLs and backlinks can take time to be discovered and how to build a practical indexing workflow without promising guaranteed search engine outcomes.

## Reader Scenario

The reader has pages, posts, backlinks, or campaign URLs that are live but not showing up in search yet. They may be a website owner with a few URLs or an agency with hundreds of client URLs.

## Article Angle

Position indexing as an operational workflow: crawlability, internal links, sitemap health, backlink discovery, and URL submission all work together. FreeIndexer is the submission and workflow layer, not a magic guarantee.

## Required Sections

1. What indexing means and why discovery can lag.
2. The difference between crawling, indexing, and ranking.
3. Pre-submission checklist: live URL, status code, robots rules, canonical tags, internal links, sitemap.
4. How FreeIndexer supports URL and backlink submission workflows.
5. Topics in this hub with links to every cluster.
6. When to use the online app, pricing plans, or desktop app.
7. FAQ about indexing expectations.
