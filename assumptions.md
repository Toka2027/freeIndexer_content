# FreeIndexer Assumptions And Validation Items

## Confirmed From The Playbook And Public Site

- FreeIndexer is a public indexing product at `https://freeindexer.com/`.
- Public surfaces include home, dashboard, pricing, buy/download app, register, login, about, and contact.
- The product is presented as `Free Indexer V2.5`.
- Monthly plans shown publicly:
  - Silver: `$5`
  - Gold: `$15`
  - Platinum: `$35`
- Desktop app page presents a one-time `$10` purchase with lifetime updates.
- Signup copy says users can get `1000 Daily points for indexer`.
- Pricing copy mentions indexer points, background indexer points, and backlinks MIX Platforms points.

## Claims That Must Be Validated Before Publication

- Home copy references submissions to `over 15,000 sites`.
- About/meta copy references submissions to `over 300 statistic sites`.
- Some public copy implies `100% indexed`.

These claims should not be repeated in article body, metadata, ads, or support material until the product owner confirms the intended wording and proof standard.

## Working Assumptions For Content Strategy

- FreeIndexer should be positioned as an indexing workflow and URL/backlink submission tool, not as a guaranteed ranking or guaranteed indexation product.
- The strongest content angle is "reduce indexing friction and create a repeatable submission workflow" rather than "promise Google will index everything."
- The desktop app is a separate commercial path for users who prefer local Windows software or repeat bulk indexing workflows.
- The online app and pricing plans are the primary recurring revenue path.
- Blog CTAs should route based on intent:
  - educational articles: register or try the online indexer
  - commercial articles: pricing page
  - desktop workflow articles: buy/download app page
  - support/troubleshooting articles: dashboard or contact
